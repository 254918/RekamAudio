# Rekam Audio - Internal Audio Recorder (Perekam Audio Internal)

![Android](https://img.shields.io/badge/Android-10%2B-green.svg) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)

**Rekam Audio** is a modern, privacy-focused Android application designed to capture **internal system audio** with high fidelity.
*(Aplikasi perekam audio internal Android yang jernih, tanpa root, dan privasi aman.)*

Unlike traditional recorders that use the microphone, Rekam Audio taps directly into the system's audio stream, ensuring crystal-clear recordings without background noise. Perfect for recording **Zoom meetings, Webinars, YouTube videos, or Podcasts**.

---

## 🇮🇩 Fitur Utama (Bahasa Indonesia)
**Mengapa memilih Rekam Audio?**
*   **Perekam Suara Internal Murni:** Merekam audio sistem langsung (Zoom, Google Meet, YouTube, Spotify) tanpa suara bising dari luar. Tidak perlu root! (Android 10+).
*   **Desain Material You:** Tampilan modern yang mengikuti warna wallpaper HP Anda.
*   **Tombol Melayang (Overlay):** Mulai/stop rekaman dari aplikasi apa saja tanpa ribet.
*   **Manajemen File Mudah:** Putar, ganti nama, dan bagikan rekaman langsung ke WhatsApp atau media sosial.
*   **Privasi Terjamin:** 100% Offline. Tidak butuh internet. Data Anda aman.

> **Kata Kunci:** Perekam rapat online, rekam kuliah online, internal audio recorder, rekam musik spotify, perekam suara zoom jernih.

## ✨ Key Features (English)

*   **📱 Pure Internal Audio**
    *   Records system sound directly (**Zoom, YouTube, Podcasts, Webinars**).
    *   No microphone interference or background noise.
    *   **Note:** Requires Android 10+ (API 29).

*   **🎨 Material You Design**
    *   Beautiful, modern UI capable of Dynamic Colors.
    *   Adapts to your device's wallpaper and theme (Light/Dark mode).
    *   Includes Themed App Icons.

*   **🔘 Floating Overlay Controls**
    *   Start and stop recordings from *any* app using a minimal floating button.
    *   No need to switch back and forth between apps to manage capture.

*   **📂 Built-in Manager**
    *   Complete management suite within the app.
    *   **Play** recordings instantly.
    *   **Rename** files for better organization.
    *   **Share** directly to social media or cloud storage.
    *   **Delete** unwanted takes.

*   **🔒 Privacy First**
    *   **100% Offline:** No internet permission required. Your recordings never leave your device unless you share them.
    *   **Transparent Permissions:** Only requests what is needed (Audio, Notification, Overlay).

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
*   **Language:** Kotlin
*   **UI Toolkit:** Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
*   **Architecture:** MVVM + Clean Architecture
*   **Audio Engine:** MediaProjection API + AudioRecord
*   **Target SDK:** Android 14 (API 34)

## 📥 Installation / Cara Install
1.  Download the latest APK from the [Releases](#) page.
    *(Unduh APK terbaru dari halaman Releases)*
2.  Install on your Android device (Android 10 or newer required).
3.  Grant the necessary permissions (Notification for controls, Overlay for the floating button).

## ⚠️ Known Issues / Masalah yang Diketahui

*   **Google Chrome & Web Browsers:** Chrome on Android generally blocks internal audio capture (except via Microphone). This is a system-level restriction by Google.
    *   **Recommendation:** Use **Samsung Internet** or **Firefox** for better compatibility with internal audio recording.
*   **Voice Calls (WhatsApp, Zoom, Meet):** Recording internal audio of voice calls is blocked by Android for privacy/security reasons.
